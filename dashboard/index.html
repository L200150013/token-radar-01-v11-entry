How a row is priced — the model card

implied = 1.2845 × TVL0.85 × growth × age × odds. Weights are fixed and locked: the scale 1.2845 and the exponent 0.85 never move. growth = 1 + 7-day change (clamped to ±30%, no report → 1.00). age = (1 + years tracked)^0.22; years from listed_at to the snapshot date, or the pool median (1.15y) when unlisted. odds = 0.55 flat — the snapshot-wide share of protocols recording a token symbol (416 of 758 = 54.9%, rounded to 0.55), the same for every row, because category cohorts are too thin for an honest per-category rate.

Verdict economics

A launch is assumed to pay a share of its implied value to farmers, split among 10,000 farmers. gross = implied × drop-share ÷ 10,000 × (horizon ÷ 180, capped at 365 days). net = gross − farm cost. Farm if net ≥ $20, Marginal if $0–$20, Skip if below $0. Move the sliders and every verdict, net and net/hour on the board updates.
